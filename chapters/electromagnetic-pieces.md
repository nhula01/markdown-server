## Physical picture

Start with the field, then ask which part of it a particular observer or experiment resolves. The notebook builds the pieces separately—electric and magnetic fields, vacuum constants, material sources, and the mathematics of flux and circulation—before assembling Maxwell’s equations.

## Key idea

In vacuum, the constants set the propagation speed

$$
c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\epsilon_0\mu_0}}.
$$

Comparing electric and magnetic effects on matter also requires the motion of the charges; comparing the numerical values of $E$ and $B$ alone misses that physical context.
