# From intuition to equations

This is the intended format for future notes in the collection.

## 1. A physical question

What are we trying to understand? Name the system and explain why the question is interesting.

## 2. A picture to think with

Begin with a sketch, a simple analogy, or a limiting case. State where that picture is useful and where it can mislead.

## 3. The essential mathematics

Introduce the quantities and assumptions before using them. Explain what the terms in an equation represent and why a particular approximation is reasonable.

## 4. Checks and connections

Ask whether the result makes sense in a familiar limit. Connect it to another topic, an experimental observable, or a research question.

## 5. What remains unclear

Keep open questions and references visible. A useful working note can show the boundary of an understanding as well as the understanding itself.
